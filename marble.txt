🎮 Pentas (New Game & Video Sidebar)

● Replaced GameSpace with Pentas — a new game and video sidebar with live telemetry and per-game tuning:
  ◦ Sidebar & Telemetry: Live FPS, CPU, GPU (`kgsl gpubusy`), and RAM tiles, thermal profile switcher, media/brightness controls, and LMOFreeform app shortcuts.
  ◦ Per-Game Tuning: Render scale via GameManager, thermal profiles mapped to `sm8450` configs, touch report rate (with HIDL TouchFeature support), and on-screen widgets.
  ◦ In-Game Call Overlay: Answer/decline buttons, auto-speakerphone, and active in-call persistence.
  ◦ Gaming Tools: Danmaku notification banners, three-finger screenshot lock, platform gesture lock (enabled by default), and game-launch memory/cache cleanup.
  ◦ Refinements: Added sidebar handle idle opacity settings, added an App Info shortcut in the per-game header, and fixed monitor/Danmaku crashes.

✨ Liquid Glass & UI Optimizations

● RenderEngine & SystemUI:
  ◦ Read Liquid Glass tuning once per frame and polled only when active.
  ◦ Cached tuning colours, reused gradients across draws, kept rims within tiny slabs, and drew rounded glows from the nine-slice mask.
● Launcher3 & Core:
  ◦ Reused Liquid Glass rim gradients and folder blur effects between draws.
  ◦ Reused floating tab bar key effects and fixed pre-draw listener leaks on glass tab bars.
● Quick Settings & Lock Screen:
  ◦ Applied a single blur region to the lock screen media player under Liquid Glass.
  ◦ Reused brightness range and stepped media volume while dragging, and prevented slider zeroing on stationary taps.
  ◦ Shared settings observers across panel elements, translated MyUI card labels, and stopped PowerMenu slider shimmer restarts outside the window.

⚙️ SystemUI, Display & Framework

● Restored Android 14 QPR1 `FingerprintInteractiveToAuth` handling and provider implementation for reliable touch-to-unlock behavior.
● Implemented status bar and navigation bar OLED burn-in protection with fixed shift direction logic.
● Redesigned per-app volume controls as inline pills inside the volume dialog with a new layered icon (and dropped the old Compose volume page in Settings).
● Enabled Lock Screen Widgets (Communal Service) and Glanceable Hub overlays for `marble`.
● Improved AOD & Doze handling:
  ◦ Deferred SurfaceFlinger refresh rate and `setDesiredMode` changes while dozing or screen-off.
  ◦ Enabled offscreen gestures to wake from AOD and briefly pulsed the display on when resuming AOD.
● Updated KeyHandler to use Pocket Judge for proximity state updates and specify wake-up reasons.
● Increased minimum visible recent tasks to 30, fixed IME switcher visibility when disabled, and disabled ART debug tracing on production builds.

🛠 Settings & SetupWizard

● Added a High Touch Polling Rate toggle (automatically disabled when Battery Saver is active).

  ◦ Allowlisted phone state and background start permissions.
